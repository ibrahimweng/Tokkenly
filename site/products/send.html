The rules, stated once.

Who you can send to
Another Tokkenly account, a crypto wallet, or a Nigerian bank account.
What it costs
The fee is on the screen before you confirm, in money — not a percentage you have to work out.
Your limit
Until your identity check is done, outgoing payments are capped. The cap is shown on the screen rather than discovered when a payment fails.
Sending shares
You can send a tokenized stock to another Tokkenly user from the holding itself.

Moving your own money between your own balances is not sending it. Converting naira to dollars inside Tokkenly does not touch your sending limit.
