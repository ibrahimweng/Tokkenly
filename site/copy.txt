Frequently asked questions
A few things you might be wondering.
What is Tokkenly?
Tokkenly is a money app that gives you access to local and US markets through tokenized stocks. Invest, share tokenized stocks through gifts and rewards, and manage everyday money with tools to receive, send, pay bills, earn, borrow, and convert between naira and stablecoins.
Which currencies can I use?
Tokkenly supports naira and stablecoins. You can convert between them to suit how you want to use your money.
Can I withdraw to my bank account?
Yes. Tokkenly lets you withdraw naira to your bank account. You can also convert stablecoins to naira before withdrawing.
Is Tokkenly only for investing?
Tokenized stocks are our main product, but Tokkenly goes further. You can also receive and send money, pay bills, earn, borrow, and convert between naira and stablecoins.
How do I get started?
Select Sign up, create your account, and complete the required checks. Add money, then explore available tokenized stocks and review their terms before investing.
Where can I get help?
Visit Help for guidance on your account, transactions, and Tokkenly products.
