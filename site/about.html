About us

Building a wider world for your money.

Our goal is to give people in Nigeria more control over what their money can do.

From everyday spending and bills to converting naira and dollars and investing in tokenized stocks listed in Nigeria and the US, we make managing money simple, open and secure — built on Base.

How we look after you

Six things we do so this feels easy.

From the day you join to the day you sell, here is what you can count on from us.

We hold the real shares for you

Every token you buy tracks a real share we hold. You get the price moves and the dividends.

We put both markets in one list

Nigerian and US companies side by side, so you pick a company rather than a market.

We show every cost up front

The amount, the fee and exactly what you get, on the screen before you press the button.

We give you the rate on the screen

Your balance sits in dollars, and the rate you are shown is the rate you get, in and out.

We tell you how it really works

Shares go up and down, and this is not a savings account. We say so on the screen, not in a footnote.
